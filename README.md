One line, at most 160 characters: a weather forecast for a city that does
not exist — absurd but believable, deadpan, like a real TV meteorologist
reading it off the map.

## The forecast

> Overnight in Quesselton: a high of 72, a low of 19, and a 40 percent chance of rain falling upward, so bring an umbrella and hold it underneath you.

148 characters. Rain falling upward is the absurd twist; the high/low, the
40 percent chance, and the practical umbrella advice keep it deadpan and
broadcast-believable, and the umbrella held underneath you is the concrete
image that makes Quesselton feel real.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt` (148 bytes, no trailing newline).
